How it works

Five steps, the same every matchday. No gut feelings, no inside tips.

  1. 1. Start from the numbers

    For each game we collect odds from many bookmakers, plus stats such as goals, shots and expected goals (xG). The betting market's average price is a very strong forecast: it already contains what thousands of people and bookmakers know.

  2. 2. Turn odds into fair probabilities

    Bookmakers build a margin into their odds, so the chances they imply add up to more than 100%. We remove that margin to get a fair chance for each outcome, and a fair price (fair odds = 1 ÷ fair chance).

    We also run our own stats model (form, attack and defence strength, xG) as a second opinion. To be honest: in our tests it hasn't beaten the market, so the picks rely on the market's fair chance, not on the model.

  3. 3. Choose one pick per game

    For each game we pick one bet whose fair odds are close to 2.0 (about a 50% chance): match result, double chance, Asian handicap, over/under 2.5 goals or both teams to score. If no single bet fits, a two-leg bet builder from the same match.

  4. 4. Only pick when the price looks generous

    We compare the fair odds with the standard prices (no boosts) at five UK bookmakers. A pick is only posted when the best price is at least 2% above the fair odds. If no bookmaker is that generous, there's no pick for that game.

  5. 5. Post at most 3 per matchday

    We post at most 3 picks per matchday: the ones where the price is furthest above fair. The picks that pass step 4 are ranked by how far the best price is above the fair odds, and the top 3 are posted. If two are level, the earlier kick-off goes first. If fewer than 3 pass, we post only those. We never fill the gap with picks that didn't pass.

    A fixed rule makes this choice, not us, so we can't pick out the ones we like.

How we keep score

Every posted pick is timestamped before kick-off, at a flat stake of 1 unit, and checked against the closing odds after the game. All posted picks, losers included, are on the Results page. Behind the scenes we also log every pick that passes step 4, not just the 3 we post, and judge the method on all of them. We're paper trading first: no real money goes on these picks until a long record shows the method works.

A 2% edge on paper is small, and prices move. Even a good method loses often. Picks near odds of 2.0 lose about half the time.
