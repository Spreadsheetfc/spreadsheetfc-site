Reading a pick

Odds
The best standard price (no boosts) among Paddy Power, William Hill, bet365, Sky Bet and Ladbrokes when we logged the pick.
Fair chance
The betting market's chance of this outcome, with the bookmakers' margin taken out.
Fair odds
1 ÷ fair chance. A pick is only posted when the best price is at least 2% above this. Odds move, so it's only worth taking if your bookmaker still offers more than the fair odds.
Published
When the pick went live on this site. Picks are never changed after publishing.

These are paper-trading picks: we log every one as if it were a real 1-unit bet, but we don't stake real money on them until the record proves itself. The method is new and unproven. Picks near odds of 2.0 win about half the time, so losing runs are normal. Bet builders carry bigger bookmaker margins.
