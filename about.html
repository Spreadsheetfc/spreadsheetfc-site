About Spreadsheet FC

A football betting project run like a spreadsheet: every number written down, every result counted.

Data over hunches

Most tips are opinions dressed up as certainty. We'd rather start from probabilities: what the numbers say each outcome is really worth, and whether a bookmaker's price is better than that.

Full transparency

Every pick is published with its odds, bookmaker, fair chance and the time it went up, always before kick-off. Picks are never edited afterwards. The website's history is public on GitHub, so anyone can check what was posted and when.

Losses shown too

Losing picks stay on the Results page alongside the winners. If the method doesn't work, the record will show that, and we'll say so.

No affiliate links

We don't earn money when you sign up to or bet with a bookmaker. There are no bookmaker affiliate links on this site.
