Play responsibly

18+ only. You must be 18 or over to gamble in the UK.

Please remember

  • Our tips are opinions, not guarantees. Many of them lose.
  • Never bet more than you can afford to lose.
  • Set a budget and a time limit before you start, and stick to them.
  • Never chase losses or bet to win back money.
  • Betting should be entertainment, not a way to make money or solve money problems.

Free, confidential help

  • BeGambleAware.org: advice, tools and support.
  • GamCare.org.uk: National Gambling Helpline 0808 8020 133 (free, 24 hours a day).
  • GAMSTOP: free self-exclusion from all UK-licensed gambling websites and apps.

If gambling stops being fun, stop. Talking to someone helps.
